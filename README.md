## Fake _ Art _ Gallery


# Welcome to the gallery! 
This is a Flask app that turns an ordinary object into a contemporary artwork. It
creates a museum tombstone, an extended interpretive label, and an exhibition
image in a gallery of the user's choosing with the OpenAI API. 

# <i> INSTRUCTIONS </i>
1. Write an object in the prompt box
2. Select a fake gallery space to exhibit your work in
3. Click generate! 


I chose these three rules to guide my AI agent when writing didactic panels for the generated works: 

<i>RULES</>
1. Use theoretical vocabulary: Replace simple, everyday words with abstract, academic terms (e.g., “meaning” → “signification” and “the viewer” → “the viewing subject”).

2. Keep interpretations open-ended: Use phrases such as “might be understood as,” “serves to interrogate,” and “opens up a space for” rather than making definitive claims.

3. Connect artwork to larger systems: Relate visual elements and artistic choices to broader concepts such as identity, power, politics, technology,  society etc.

<i>RULES (explained)</i>
First, the structure is very important. By using larger words and phrases, it establishes the interpretive voice as one of authority, implying the education level and competence of the writer or docent. 
Second, being explicit kills the artwork; the best interpretations leave room for imagination and subjectivity with minimal curator bias in exact statements. 
Third, I wanted the AI to pull on its own resources and training in the arts, as it has access largely to all human knowledge. By creating this connection, it positions the artwork in a broader context, expanding beyond the web and relating to the material world. 
Further, I added two bystanders who add dumb commentary to the scene, a satire of the viewers not understanding the alienating language in the didactic panel.  

<i>IMAGE PROMPT & LABEL</i>
The image prompt does not have access to the original user prompt, it is derived solely from the label and art description. By generating an image from inflated, over-detailed language in a gallery context, the image comes out looking more like an art piece than a typical object. 

<i>REFLECTION</i>
I believe the model is so fluent in speaking IAE language because of the content it was trained on and how it "thinks." OpenAI is an American company trained largly on English work, with its training set heavy on academic, artistic, and formally published works to mimic how humans speak. This Western lens gives it an advantage when interpreting English prompts heavy on culturally Western material references.
